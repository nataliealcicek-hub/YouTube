# Quote Request Email — Private Label 2-Ply Bath Tissue

**How to use this file**

1. Send the master email below to all four converters *simultaneously*, not sequentially. Parallel quotes are how you get comparable numbers and real leverage.
2. Swap in the per-converter opening line from the section underneath — it shows you did your homework and materially improves response rates.
3. Fill the four `[BRACKETED]` fields before sending.
4. Log every response in the **Supplier Tracker** tab of `TP_Sourcing_and_Landed_Cost_Model.xlsx`.

**A note on tone.** This is written as a buyer who knows the category — it cites parent roll economics and names a target cost band. That is deliberate. Converters quote differently to someone who knows what fiber costs than to someone who doesn't. Do not soften it.

---

## MASTER EMAIL

**Subject:** Private Label 2-Ply Bath Tissue — RFQ, New Retail/DTC Brand

---

[PER-CONVERTER OPENING LINE — see below]

I'm developing a private label 2-ply bath tissue brand for budget-conscious US consumers, sold direct-to-consumer and through online retail. I'm requesting quotes from a short list of converters and would like to include you.

**Product specification**

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Product | 2-ply bath tissue, value grade |
| Sheet size | 4.0" × 4.0" (full size — this is a firm spec, not a target) |
| Sheets per roll | 300 (33.3 sq ft per roll) |
| Basis weight | ~14 gsm per ply / 28 gsm total, or your nearest standard value grade |
| Fiber | Virgin, or virgin/recycled blend — please quote both if you run both |
| Core | Standard 1.6", unprinted |
| Winding | **Compressed / high-density preferred** — please quote compressed and standard separately |
| Overwrap | Paper preferred; please quote poly as an alternate |
| Pack counts | 12 / 24 / 30 / 36 roll |
| Carton | Retail carton designed as a ship-ready shipper (direct label application, no overbox) |
| Certifications | Septic-safe and RV-safe testing/certification if you can support it |

**What I need quoted**

1. **Ex-works price per roll** at 10,000 / 50,000 rolls / 1 truckload / 2 truckloads.
2. **Your minimum order quantity for a new brand's first run** — including whether you'll go below your published MOQ for an initial production run. This is a significant factor in my decision.
3. **One-time costs:** plate/cylinder charges, artwork setup, die/tooling for the shipper carton.
4. **Lead time** from artwork approval to shipment, for a first run and for repeat runs.
5. **Cube comparison:** finished pack dimensions (L × W × H) and pallet configuration, compressed vs. standard wind. Because this product ships parcel to consumers, dimensional weight drives my landed cost more than roll price does — I need real carton dimensions, not estimates.
6. **Rolls per pallet and pallets per trailer** on the 30-roll configuration.
7. **Fulfillment:** will you hold finished branded inventory and ship pallets to my 3PL on release, or is it strictly ship-on-production?
8. **Samples:** what you can send against the spec above, and lead time for a custom sample run.

**Commercial context, so you can quote sharply**

I'm modeling against current parent roll economics — recycled/mixed pulp in the $900–1,050/short ton range and standard virgin at $950–1,200 — which puts my target ex-works cost in the **$0.18–$0.24 per roll** band for this spec. If your quote lands materially outside that, I'd welcome a note on what's driving the difference; it may be that I've specced something more expensive than I intended, and I'd rather adjust the spec than lose a good converter over it.

I'm aware capacity utilization has softened this year. I'm looking for a long-term converting partner, not a one-off run, and I'd rather build volume with one supplier than split it.

**Timeline**

Quotes by **[DATE — give them 2 weeks]**. Samples reviewed the following week, converter selected the week after, first production run targeted for **[MONTH]**.

Happy to sign an NDA before we get into specifics, and happy to get on a call if that's faster than email.

Thank you,

**[YOUR NAME]**

## THE SEPARATE EMAIL YOU ALSO NEED TO SEND

The converters above will not ship single packs to your customers. Your fulfillment cost is a bigger variable than your roll price, and it has to be sourced in parallel — not after you've picked a converter.

Send this to **three regional paper distributors and two DTC 3PLs** the same week.

**Subject:** Blind-Ship Fulfillment — Branded Bath Tissue, DTC

> I'm launching a private label bath tissue brand shipping direct to consumers, and I'm looking for a fulfillment partner to hold branded inventory and ship to my customers under my brand — with no reference to your company on the outer carton or packing slip.
>
> Volumes to start: roughly [X] orders per month, 30-roll packs, ~7 lb actual weight and ~12 lb dimensional. The retail carton is designed as the shipper, so there's no overboxing or void fill — pick, label, ship.
>
> Please quote:
> 1. Receiving (per pallet), storage (per pallet per month), and pick & pack (per order).
> 2. Your parcel rates at 12–15 lb billed weight, zones 2 through 8.
> 3. **Your DIM divisor.** This product is bulky and light, so the divisor materially changes my economics. If you can offer 166 rather than 139, say so — it's a deciding factor.
> 4. Whether you can blind-ship under my brand.
> 5. Whether you have East and West coast locations, or a partner network that does.
>
> One requirement worth flagging up front: I need a monthly export showing length, width, height and **billed** weight per shipment. Without that I have no way to diagnose packaging decisions, and I'd rather agree it now than ask for it later.
>
> Thank you,
> **[YOUR NAME]**

**Why this email matters:** blind-ship fulfillment is almost never advertised as a product, but paper distributors already handle cube-heavy goods daily and their handling cost is typically below a DTC-specialist 3PL's. Most will say yes if asked directly. Nobody will offer it unprompted.

## WHAT TO DO WITH THE ANSWERS

| Answer received | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Ex-works $/roll | Supplier Tracker cols I–K, then the winner goes in **Assumptions → "ACTUAL QUOTED ex-works"** |
| Quoted MOQ | Supplier Tracker col H |
| Carton dimensions, compressed vs standard | Recompute **Assumptions → "Compression saving"** from real cube |
| Pallet/trailer config | **Assumptions → rolls per pallet / pallets per trailer** |
| 3PL pick & pack, storage | **Assumptions → section 5** |
| Carrier rates by zone | Overwrite the entire **Freight Rates** tab |
| DIM divisor | **Assumptions → DIM divisor** |

Once those are in, the **Scenario — Pack × Zone** tab tells you your real pack size and your real price. Everything before that is a placeholder.
