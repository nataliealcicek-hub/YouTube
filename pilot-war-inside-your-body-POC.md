## Style bible (locked via one style-key image)
- **World:** interior of the human body as a vast, moody biological battlefield — glowing blood-vessel tunnels like cathedral caves.
- **Hero character:** a brave, friendly **macrophage** — rounded teal-and-white blob with two dot eyes and stubby pseudopods (the recurring channel mascot).
- **Villains:** spiky bacteria in sickly green / acid yellow with red rims.
- **Palette:** crimson + electric teal + bioluminescent gold.
- **Render:** mixed-media collage (cut-paper + gouache + ink), flat 2D depth, strictly non-photorealistic. No on-screen text (captions added at assembly if desired).

## The 12 blocks (narration + visual)

| # | Narration (VO) | Visual (Seedance clip) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Right now, you feel fine. Calm, safe, maybe a little bored. But deep inside your body, thousands of tiny soldiers are dying to keep you exactly this comfortable. | Slow push-in toward a peaceful sleeping human silhouette; dust motes in warm light. |
| 2 | Every single day, without you noticing a thing, your body fights a full-scale war. And today, for once, you are going to watch one battle up close. | Camera dives through the skin into the glowing vessel-world; epic reveal. |
| 3 | It begins with something small. A splinter. A scratch. A gap in your skin barely wider than a single human hair. To you, nothing. To them, a gate. | A dark splinter pierces the translucent skin-wall, tearing a jagged gap; light spills in. |
| 4 | Because through that tiny opening, an army is already pouring in. Bacteria — alien, hungry, and fast — spilling into the warm world beneath your skin. | Spiky green bacteria flood through the gap like invaders storming a gate. |
| 5 | And these invaders have a terrifying gift. Every twenty minutes, each one splits in two. One becomes two, two become four, four become eight. | Macro: one bacterium splits into two, then four — sinister exponential division. |
| 6 | Left alone for a single day, that handful becomes millions. This is a race against time, and the clock has already started. But your body just noticed. | Wide: the vessel teeming with green; a faint teal glow awakens in the distance. |
| 7 | First to arrive are your macrophages. Living creatures that fight in the strangest way imaginable. They do not shoot or stab. They hunt by swallowing the enemy whole. | The teal hero macrophage surges in and engulfs a bacterium whole. |
| 8 | One macrophage can devour dozens of invaders, engulfing them completely into its own body. It is messy, brutal, and for a while, it actually works. | Several macrophages sweep through the swarm, feeding, glowing brighter. |
| 9 | But there are too many. For every invader eaten, more keep flooding through the wound. The macrophages are outnumbered, and slowly, they begin to lose. | A lone macrophage encircled by an overwhelming green mass; it dims, faltering. |
