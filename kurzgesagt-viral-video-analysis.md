## 2. The Viral Archetypes — the 6 patterns that repeat

Nearly every Kurzgesagt breakout falls into one of these. This is the actionable part.

### Archetype A — The Cosmic "What If You…" (highest hit-rate)
*Black holes, falling into the sun, Earth kidnapped by a black hole, largest star.*
- **Mechanic:** second-person + a physically impossible scenario your brain can't stop simulating.
- **Why it spreads:** universal curiosity, zero prerequisite knowledge, awe + fear combo, translates across every language/culture.
- **Emotion:** wonder + dread.

### Archetype B — The Existential Mystery
*Fermi Paradox, Great Filter, simulation argument, is death real, are we alone.*
- **Mechanic:** poses a genuinely unresolved question about reality or humanity's fate, then refuses easy comfort.
- **Why it spreads:** it becomes an argument. People share it to *debate*, not just to inform. Comment sections explode.
- **Emotion:** unsettled fascination.

### Archetype C — Your Body Is an Epic / Body Horror
*Immune system, parasites, viruses, cancer, "you are a colony."*
- **Mechanic:** reframe your own body as a battlefield, a city, or a horror movie happening right now inside you.
- **Why it spreads:** it's *about the viewer* — maximally personal — plus disgust and fear are high-arousal, high-share emotions.
- **Emotion:** "that's happening in ME right now?!"

### Archetype D — The Forbidden / Provocative Question
*Detonate all nukes, nuke a city, gruesome ways to die.*
- **Mechanic:** ask the taboo question everyone's curious about, then answer it soberly and responsibly.
- **Why it spreads:** the title alone is a curiosity gap you can't resist clicking; the responsible execution earns the "actually smart" share.
- **Emotion:** guilty curiosity → respect.

### Archetype E — Pure Scale / Perspective
*Size comparisons, deep time, how big is the universe, how small is an atom.*
- **Mechanic:** relentless zoom in or out until the viewer's sense of proportion breaks.
- **Why it spreads:** hypnotic, rewatchable, minimal narration = travels globally, satisfying dopamine of "whoa."
- **Emotion:** vertigo + awe.

### Archetype F — The Emotional / Philosophical Departure (rare, huge)
*The Egg, Optimistic Nihilism, Loneliness.*
- **Mechanic:** drop the "explainer" mode entirely and tell a story or make a meaning-of-life argument.
- **Why it spreads:** it hits people *emotionally*, not intellectually — so it's shared as "this changed how I think," the highest-value share.
- **Emotion:** catharsis / comfort / chills.

## 3. The Common DNA — what every viral one shares

Across all six archetypes, the breakouts consistently do these things:

1. **The topic is the spectacle.** No clickbait screaming. The *idea* is inherently huge (death, the universe, your body, the end of everything).
2. **Second person, present tense.** "Right now, in your body…" / "Imagine you fall…" It's always about *you*.
3. **Scale whiplash.** Tiny ↔ cosmic in one breath. This is their single most repeated device.
4. **A concrete metaphor for every abstract idea.** Entropy = a cooling coffee. The immune system = a city under siege. Never cold jargon.
5. **High-arousal emotions.** Awe, fear, disgust, existential dread, catharsis — *not* mild interest. Arousal drives sharing; calm interest doesn't.
6. **A curiosity-gap title + one clean iconic thumbnail.** A question or bold claim, plus a single Kurzgesagt-bird + one visual metaphor on flat color. No text clutter.
7. **An optimistic or awe-struck landing.** Even the scary ones resolve on wonder or agency ("optimistic nihilism"), which makes them feel good to share.
8. **Language-agnostic visuals.** Heavy on animation and scale, light on culture-specific references → travels worldwide and gets re-dubbed.
9. **Evergreen, not newsy** (with the COVID exception). Black holes and death don't expire, so views compound for years — most of these went "viral" slowly over time, not overnight.

---

## 4. The One Exception That Proves the Rule

**The Coronavirus video (their #1)** is their *only* mega-hit that was **timely rather than evergreen.** It won by being the clear, calm, authoritative explainer during a week of global panic when nothing else existed. Lesson: their normal strategy is evergreen compounding, but when a massive, scary, universal news event hits *and* they can be first-and-best, that's their single biggest spike. It's rare and not repeatable on demand — the evergreen archetypes (A–F) are the repeatable playbook.

---

## 5. How to Use This (applying it to a new video)

To maximize viral odds on a Kurzgesagt-style video, stack the traits:
- **Pick from Archetype A, B, or C** (highest hit-rate: cosmic what-if, existential mystery, or body-as-epic).
- **Make the title a curiosity gap** the viewer can't leave unclicked.
- **Open in second person, present tense**, with immediate scale whiplash.
- **Target one high-arousal emotion** (awe / dread / disgust) and commit to it.
- **Land on wonder + agency**, not despair, so it feels good to share.
- **Keep visuals language-agnostic** so it can travel and be dubbed.
